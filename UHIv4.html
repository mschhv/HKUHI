[01] THERMAL MECHANICS OVERVIEW

What Creates Hong Kong's Heat Trap?

// 01. ABSORPTION

Low Albedo Concrete

Hong Kong's extensive dark concrete, asphalt roads, and building facades absorb high solar radiation during the daytime, storing thermal energy instead of reflecting it back into space.

// 02. ANTHROPOGENIC HEAT

AC Exhaust & Traffic

Dense clusters of high-rise air conditioning condenser units pump massive volumes of hot waste air directly into narrow, poorly ventilated street canyons like Sham Shui Po.

// 03. IMPERMEABILITY

Lost Evapotranspiration

Impermeable urban concrete prevents rainwater infiltration into soil. With sparse vegetation, natural evaporative cooling is almost completely absent in commercial hubs.

[03] HONG KONG URBAN PROFILE

District Microclimates: Mong Kok to Victoria Harbour

[HIGH-DENSITY URBAN CORE] MONG KOK / SHAM SHUI PO

Street Canyons & Heat Traps

In Mong Kok, building heights greatly exceed street widths. This narrow aspect ratio traps thermal radiation and prevents coastal sea breezes from Victoria Harbour from flushing out accumulated heat during hot summer afternoons.

Night Temp Elevation: +4.0°C to +8.0°C
[WALL-EFFECT ARCHITECTURE] VICTORIA HARBOURFRONT

Barrier High-Rises

Continuous rows of high-rise residential towers along coastal reclamation areas act as solid concrete walls, blocking air corridors and depriving inland districts of essential marine cooling ventilation.

Wind Velocity Loss: Up to 60% Inland Reduction
[04] ACTION PLAYBOOK

Hong Kong Cooling Solutions

// ACTION 01

A/C Efficiency & Eco Temperatures

Set household air conditioners to 25.5°C alongside ceiling fans. Each 1°C increase saves ~6% energy and reduces hot waste heat exhausted into street corridors.

Impact: Immediate Waste Heat Reduction
// ACTION 02

Balcony & Roof Greening

Plant potted foliage and vertical climbing plants on balconies to absorb direct sunlight before it warms concrete building envelopes.

Impact: Lowers Indoor Wall Temp by 2.0°C–4.0°C
// POLICY 01

Urban Air Corridor Mandates

Enforce Planning Department Air Ventilation Assessments (AVA) to preserve wind gaps between buildings along coastal corridors.

Strategy: Ventilation & Airflow
// POLICY 02

Cool Pavements & Reflective Roofs

Apply light-colored reflective coatings on public footpaths to reduce surface radiation in high-density estates.

Strategy: Albedo Enhancement
