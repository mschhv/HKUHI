[01] THERMAL MECHANICS OVERVIEW
// 01. ABSORPTION

Low Albedo Materials

Hong Kong's extensive dark concrete, asphalt roads, and building facades absorb high solar radiation during the daytime, storing thermal energy instead of reflecting it.

// 02. ANTHROPOGENIC HEAT

AC Exhaust & Traffic

Dense clusters of high-rise air conditioning condenser units pump massive volumes of waste heat directly into narrow, poorly ventilated street canyons like Sham Shui Po.

// 03. IMPERMEABILITY

Lost Evapotranspiration

Impermeable urban concrete prevents rainwater infiltration into the soil. With sparse vegetation, natural evaporative cooling is almost completely absent in commercial hubs.

[03] HONG KONG URBAN PROFILE & WALL BUILDINGS

District Microclimates: Mong Kok to Tai Mo Shan

[HIGH-DENSITY URBAN CORE] MONG KOK / SHAM SHUI PO

Street Canyons & Heat Traps

In Mong Kok, building heights greatly exceed street widths. This narrow aspect ratio traps thermal radiation and prevents coastal sea breezes from Victoria Harbour from flushing out accumulated heat and pollution during hot summer afternoons.

Night Temp Elevation: +4.0°C to +8.0°C
[WALL-EFFECT ARCHITECTURE] VICTORIA HARBOURFRONT

Barrier High-Rises

Continuous rows of high-rise residential towers along coastal reclamation areas act as solid concrete walls, blocking air corridors. This deprives inland districts like Kowloon City of essential marine cooling ventilation.

Wind Velocity Loss: Up to 60% Inland Reduction
[04] ACTION PLAYBOOK

Hong Kong Cooling Solutions

// ACTION 01

A/C Efficiency & Eco Temperatures

Set household air conditioners to 25.5°C alongside ceiling fans. Each 1°C increase saves ~6% energy and reduces hot waste heat exhausted into street corridors.

Impact: Immediate Waste Heat Reduction
// ACTION 02

Balcony & Roof Greening

Plant potted foliage and vertical climbing plants on balconies to absorb direct sunlight before it warms concrete building envelopes.

Impact: Lowers Indoor Wall Temp by 2°C–4°C
// ACTION 03

Solar Reflective Window Films

Apply high-albedo solar shading films on glass windows facing south and west to block infrared solar gain during peak summer hours.

Impact: Cuts AC Cooling Demand
// ACTION 04

Permeable Outdoor Surfaces

For private garden plots or village houses in the New Territories, replace solid concrete paving with porous grass pavers to allow water evaporation.

Impact: Restores Natural Infiltration
// POLICY 01

Urban Air Corridor Mandates

Enforce HKSAR Planning Department air ventilation assessments (AVA) to preserve wind gaps between buildings along coastal corridors.

Strategy: Ventilation & Airflow
// POLICY 02

Cool Pavements & Reflective Roofs

Apply light-colored reflective pavement coatings on public footpaths and basketball courts to reduce surface radiation in high-density estates.

Strategy: Albedo Enhancement
// POLICY 03

Blue-Green Infrastructure

Expand urban parks, bio-swales, and artificial wetlands in new development zones like Kai Tak and the Northern Metropolis.

Strategy: CEDD / DSD Urban Greening
// POLICY 04

District Cooling Systems (DCS)

Implement centralized seawater-cooled district cooling systems (such as in Kai Tak) to eliminate individual rooftop AC waste heat units.

Strategy: 35% Energy Reduction

HELP COOL HONG KONG'S MICROCLIMATE
